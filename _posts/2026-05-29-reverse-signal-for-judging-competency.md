Speaking of the [_output-competence decoupling_](output-competence-decoupling.html), this is a signal that can be used in reverse to figure out what you can improve:

> [**Planktonne**](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48315309): A general pattern for LLMs is that they look really good at things you are bad at. What that means is that if you find yourself thinking of its output as _significantly_ better than yours in a particular domain, there's a high chance that you are not equipped to judge that quality effectively.

This [HN thread](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48313810) on [Various LLM Smells](https://shvbsle.in/various-llm-smells/) has a few other choice thoughts:

> [**pydry**](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48316520): Companies that don't care about code quality always care about the side effects of poor code quality. They just can't connect the dots.
